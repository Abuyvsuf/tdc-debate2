National Finals

THE PREMIER STAGE

Nationals

TDC National Debate Finals 2025

Aug 2–3, 2025 · Nairobi

The 2025 season culminated with regional champions, educators, financial sector leaders, and public officials gathered for TDC's premier event. KDIC Director of Bank Resolution David Irungu addressed students on financial literacy as a national development strategy. Jamhuri High School emerged as 2025 National Champions. Topics spanned savings culture, insurance awareness, economic inclusion, and youth in Kenya's financial future. The weekend closed with TDC's signature African Themed Gala Night.
