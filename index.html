“We started with one room, a handful of students, and a belief that young Kenyans had important things to say.”

TDC began as a small after-school forum in Nairobi. Today it spans 12 counties, 40+ schools, and thousands of students debating everything from SDG 13 (Climate Action) to SDG 8 (Decent Work) — building a generation that thinks before it speaks and speaks when it matters.
