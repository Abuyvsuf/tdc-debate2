About the Team

THE PEOPLE BEHIND THE CIRCLE

Educators, youth leaders, and civic partners building the next generation of thinkers.

“TDC began as a small after-school forum. Today it shapes how thousands of young Kenyans think about the world.”

Building an informed citizenry one student at a time through debates and conversations, The Debate Circle works with 14–18 year-olds and university students across Kenya. TDC spans 12 active regions, 40+ schools, 3,200+ students, and 90+ mentors, with every debate framed around the 17 UN Sustainable Development Goals and the UN Global Goals alignment.
