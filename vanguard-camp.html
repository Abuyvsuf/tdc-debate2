What is Vanguard?

PRACTICE, FEEDBACK, AND CONFIDENCE

Vanguard is a focused TDC camp for students who want structured practice beyond tournament day. It supports debate fundamentals, communication confidence, teamwork, and reflective learning without presenting the camp as invite-only or application-only.
